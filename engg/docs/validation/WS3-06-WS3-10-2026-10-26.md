## Also checked in this gate (case-file safety)
- **J5 masking:** the case list shows the ward, never the address (`listPlace`). Phone and Aadhaar-like numbers are masked in narratives (`maskPii`). Non-named person roles are shown by description only (`personLabel`).
- **J4 audit:** FIR filing, evidence, MO reviews, person links, diary, status changes and appeals write `audit_log`, which is append-only ([D-030](../DECISIONS.md), 0007_03).
- **RLS:** other-station officers cannot write a case, upload to it, link people to it or publish its appeal. Each is covered by a rolled-back RLS-negative test (0007_04, 0007_06, 0007_08, 0007_09) and the live `evidence-check.mjs`.
